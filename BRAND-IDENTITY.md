# TrustRadar — Brand Identity

## Recommended product naming

- **Primary product name:** TrustRadar
- **Formal descriptor:** All-in-One OSINT & Threat Investigation Platform
- **Product shorthand / package identity:** TrustRadar AIO
- **Tagline:** Discover. Investigate. Connect.

## Why this naming system

“TrustRadar” is compact, pronounceable and memorable. “Trust” signals careful evaluation and evidence quality; “Radar” signals discovery, visibility and monitoring. The descriptor explains the product category without forcing the generic abbreviation “AIO” into the most prominent brand position.

Keep “TrustRadar AIO” in package identifiers, technical filenames and places where the all-in-one positioning is useful. Use “TrustRadar” as the primary logo wordmark and product name in presentations.

## Brand promise

A workspace for organizing public-source research, evaluating evidence and documenting investigative reasoning — not an automated truth oracle.

## Voice and interface principles

- Clear, calm, precise and evidence-led.
- Never label a person or organization malicious based on a single weak signal.
- Separate observed data, analyst interpretation and untested hypothesis.
- Show provenance, retrieval time, confidence and limitations.
- Avoid sensational threat language and unsupported risk scores.
